## 👩‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Computer Science graduate** from the Islamic University of Gaza 🇵🇸 (**Excellent with Honor, 95.71/100**). My work sits where **data, education, and humanitarian impact** meet.

- 📊 **MEAL Assistant @ Theatre Day Productions**: monitoring and evaluating a UNICEF-funded psychosocial support project for children in Gaza schools (pre/post assessments, indicator tracking, narrative reporting).
- 🎓 **Teaching Assistant @ IUG**: teaching Programming Fundamentals, Python, Databases, and Advanced Java.
- 🧑‍🏫 **Instructor @ MET Academy**: teaching Python for Data Analysis and Operating Systems.
- 📈 **Previously: MEAL & Data Analyst @ Ard El Insan**: cleaned and analyzed humanitarian program data, built Power BI and Tableau dashboards, and automated reporting with Power Query, Excel, and VBA.

> 💡 I'm open to **Data Analyst** and **MEAL** opportunities, research collaborations, and remote roles.
